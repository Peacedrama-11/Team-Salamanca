---
name: Career Crash Test policy
description: User intent and non-predictive scope for AI adoption scenario comparisons.
---

The Career Crash Test is an illustrative reflection exercise about how work could evolve under gradual AI adoption versus faster workflow redesign. The user requested predefined content and simple matching rules, not LLMs, machine learning or external APIs. Do not present fabricated job-risk scores or automation probabilities.

**Why:** The user's goal is to help people understand possible work changes and identify skills to develop, not to forecast individual employment outcomes.

**How to apply:** Keep future scenario content hypothetical and retain the explicit disclaimer that results are not personalized forecasts or predictions based on actual labor-market data.
